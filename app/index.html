How it works

1. Pick one of nine layouts, then a photo for each panel — use the six shipped samples or upload your own. 2. Drag a panel's photo to move it, scroll to zoom, or use the sliders. 3. Write the title, subtitle, tagline and place stamp, pick a lettering style and a colour grade, and switch the poster art (sun, palms, skyline, frame, grain) on or off. 4. Choose a post size and hit Export PNG. Your project is saved in this browser automatically, so a reload keeps your work.
