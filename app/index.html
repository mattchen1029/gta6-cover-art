How it works

1. Pick a layout, then a photo for each panel — use the shipped samples or upload your own. 2. Drag a panel's photo to move it, scroll to zoom, or use the sliders. 3. Write your caption, pick a style, colour and grade. 4. Choose a post size and hit Export PNG. Your project is saved in this browser automatically, so a reload keeps your work.
